The studio

Artist-engineer, physics first

Robert trained in physics and engineering, then spent years making large-scale projection and audiovisual work. Memetic Media is where the two halves meet.

Real-time visuals come from the same equations that grow coral and fingerprints. Those patterns become printable objects. And on the systems side, multi-agent workflows split a job into small, self-checking steps, then stop for a human before anything irreversible goes out.

  • Visuals: reaction-diffusion and quasicrystal fields, VJ loops, projection mapping.
  • Objects: 3D print, resin and laser, from measured desk tools to pattern-grown forms.
  • Systems: human-gated AI agents, spreadsheet rescue, ops dashboards.
  • Instrument: MORPHOS, a living field you can touch and hear.
